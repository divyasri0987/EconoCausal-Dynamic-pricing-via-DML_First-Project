## Day 4: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Today, I focused on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) for the EconoCausal – Dynamic Pricing via Double Machine Learning project.

The objective of this step is to understand the dataset, examine the distribution of important variables, and explore patterns related to discounts and customer purchasing behaviour.

EDA helps identify trends, relationships, and potential data quality issues before applying machine learning and causal inference techniques.

The findings from this analysis will help guide the next stages of the project, including Double Machine Learning and discount budget optimization.

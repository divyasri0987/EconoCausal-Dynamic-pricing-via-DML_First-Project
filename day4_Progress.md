## Day 4: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Today, I focused on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) for the EconoCausal – Dynamic Pricing via Double Machine Learning project.The objective of this step is to understand the dataset, examine the distribution of important variables, and explore patterns related to discounts and customer purchasing behaviour.EDA helps identify trends, relationships, and potential data quality issues before applying machine learning and causal inference techniques.The findings from this analysis will help guide the next stages of the project, including Double Machine Learning and discount budget optimization.

During this stage, I focused on understanding the dataset and identifying the key variables required for the project.

I explored the relationship between discount rates, product prices, and customer purchasing behaviour to understand how these factors may be connected.

The analysis will help identify useful patterns and determine which variables can be considered for further modelling.

I also reviewed the dataset structure to understand the distribution of values and identify any inconsistencies that may affect the analysis.

These observations will provide a foundation for the next phase of the project, which involves causal effect estimation using Double Machine Learning.

The insights gained from EDA will also support future work on optimizing discount allocation within a limited marketing budget.
